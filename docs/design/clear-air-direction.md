# Clear Air — direction contract

**THESIS:** A daylight instrument, not a dark console. The screen is a window: network haze is the filtered path, and connecting clears it. It refuses the centered glowing orb, the shield, the neon gradient, and every claim the data cannot back.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm atmospheric mist (`#E9F0EC`) into paper (`#F7F4EC`), deep green-black ink (`#0E1B1D`), one horizon teal (`#0E7C86`), and matte paper surfaces separated by 1px hairlines. Schibsted Grotesk speaks; Geist Mono measures. Thin 1.5px line icons, a cast sun disc, painted haze strata, no glass, no glow, no ring motif. Night Air replaces the ground with `#0B1416` and lifts the teal to `#4FB3BF`.

**STORY:** The user sees the real network, the real state, and one obvious control. Tap: the haze clears outward from the aperture, a teal line sweeps the horizon, and the sun rises only after traffic crosses the tunnel. The label is believed because it is never shown early. Next: pick a faster relay or measure the path.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Top-left mono rail with network and clock (session time when live). A 34px status word beneath it, left aligned. A full-bleed horizon at 38% height. A 96px aperture on the horizon, left of centre — the only large object. Below: a three-figure data shelf (ping, down, up) with protocol, relay, and trust tier. A destination row above a floating three-destination nav plus a separate settings action. The aperture is the primary action; the destination row opens the relay sheet.

**FORM:** Horizon-and-aperture instrument (ordered-list position 3 of 6; seed key `21d0bb8f`). One authored moment: haze dissipation, horizon sweep, sun rise. Spring and opacity only; reduced motion cross-fades.

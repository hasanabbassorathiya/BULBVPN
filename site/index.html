Introduction

The tunnel you can inspect.

BULBVPN is a lean Android VPN. One tap connects you through a community OpenVPN endpoint — and instead of asking you to trust a green padlock, it puts the evidence on screen: your public exit IP, the server you landed on, and a session log written line by line.

How it works

Four steps. Nothing hidden.

Most VPN apps ask for faith. BULBVPN asks you to look. Every stage of the connection leaves a visible trace you can read, copy, or verify against a third party of your choosing.

01

Connect

One tap on the orb. The app pulls the live community endpoint list, picks a server with a valid OpenVPN profile, and negotiates the tunnel — with each step reported in the in-app log as it happens.

02

Verify

The moment the tunnel is up, your public exit IP appears on the home screen — country, address, and a copy button. Paste it into any lookup service and check the claim yourself.

03

Prove it

Run the built-in throughput check against Cloudflare's edge: latency, jitter, packet loss, download and upload — measured live through the tunnel, not estimated from a marketing slide.

04

Read the tape

Every handshake, retry, and state change is appended to a plain-text session log on the device. When a connection behaves oddly, the evidence is already there — see the screens.

The shape of it

Small surface, honest numbers.

No accounts, no subscription wall, no browsing history leaving the phone. What the app does send — connection events to Firebase Analytics, an exit-IP lookup to Cloudflare, the endpoint list from a public mirror — is itemised in the privacy policy.

Endpoints 96 NODES LIVE

Community-run VPN Gate servers across dozens of countries, refreshed from the public list on every connection attempt. Slow nodes happen; the log will tell you which one you got.

Time to tunnel 1 TAP

Tap the orb, watch the states, get the exit IP. No forms, no sign-in.

Measurement 8 PROBES

Latency packets, staged download and upload passes against Cloudflare's edge.

Disclosure

What runs, and what it talks to

The app integrates Firebase Analytics and connects to four kinds of remote services: the VPN endpoint itself, a public server list mirror, Cloudflare's speed and trace endpoints, and Google's connectivity probe. Each is named in the privacy policy — read it before you connect, not after.

Premium

Same tunnel.
Fewer distractions.

Premium is a paid tier on top of the same client. It removes the banner ad and moves the least-congested relays to the top of the list. The free tier keeps every relay — Premium does not take servers away from anyone.

What changes

Honest list, both ways.

Premium is deliberately small. The tunnel, the relay list, the exit IP panel, and the speed checks are identical on both tiers.

01

Included

Ad-free browsing inside the app, and a ranking boost for relays with lower measured latency and fewer recent failures on your network.

02

Not included

No exclusive servers, no higher bandwidth cap, no different privacy terms. Premium does not promise a speed your ISP does not deliver, and it does not make traffic invisible.

03

Free tier

Every relay, country filter, auto-select, exit IP readout, and speed test stays available without paying. Free shows one banner ad while the app is open.

04

Restore and cancel

Purchases are tied to the Apple ID or Google account that bought them. Use “Restore purchases” in the app after reinstalling, and cancel from the store's subscription settings.

Launch codes

Redeem a code in the app.

Premium codes are redeemed inside the app: Settings → Premium → Redeem code. Store-issued codes (App Store offer codes and Google Play promo codes) are checked by the store at redemption time.

App Store

iPhone codes

Apple offer codes for the monthly or yearly plan. Codes are single use and expire on the date printed on the offer.

Google Play

Android codes

Play promo codes for the monthly or yearly plan. Redeem from the Play Store checkout or inside the app.

Cross-platform

Universal codes

A small set of codes that work on either platform. They are validated by our backend and grant the same Premium entitlement.

The launch batch is distributed when version 1.1.0 reaches the stores. No code list is published before the stores finish reviewing the subscriptions, because codes cannot be generated until then.

BULB VPN is a free, unlimited VPN and WiFi security app with nothing hidden. No account. No subscription. No ads. No activity logs.

ONE SCREEN, EVERYTHING VISIBLE
Your real exit IP sits on the home screen — tap to copy. Live session time and transfer rates update as you browse. A session log is stored on your device only, so you always know what happened while you were connected.

WHY BULB VPN
• Free forever — every feature and every server, no paywall
• No account required — install and connect in one tap
• Exit IP on screen — see the IP you are browsing with, always
• On-device session log — connection events stay on your phone, never uploaded
• Community servers worldwide — hundreds of free community VPN endpoints, ranked by ping
• Speed test — measure download, upload, ping, packet loss and jitter through the VPN tunnel
• OpenVPN protocol — the audited, industry-standard tunnel
• Dark modern interface — live status, country flags and latency per server

PRIVATE & SECURE WIFI
Connect on public hotspots to encrypt your traffic and keep your browsing private on untrusted networks. BULB VPN works as a WiFi shield wherever you are.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open the app — your current public IP is shown
2. Tap to connect — auto-select the fastest server, or pick a country
3. Watch your exit IP change the moment the tunnel is up
4. Run the built-in speed test any time for real numbers

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
No account, no connection logs. Server lists come from a public mirror; only core reliability events are measured anonymously.
